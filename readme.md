# 🧠 AI Engineering – From Fundamentals to Real-World Applications

**Understanding how modern AI systems work, one concept at a time.**

Welcome to my AI Engineering Learning Journey!

This repository documents my ongoing exploration of the architecture, fundamentals, technologies, and engineering principles behind modern AI systems.

Rather than treating AI as a black box, my goal is to understand what happens behind the scenes — how Large Language Models process information, how tokens and embeddings work, how Transformers generate responses, how Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) connects LLMs with external knowledge, and how AI agents perform tasks using tools and workflows.

## 📚 Learning Approach

Every concept will be explored through three perspectives:

1. **Understand the Theory:** Learn what it is, why it exists, and the problem it solves.
2. **Understand the Internals:** Explore how it works behind the scenes, including architectures, algorithms, and workflows.
3. **Build Practically:** Implement concepts using Python, experiment with different approaches, and connect them to real-world AI applications.
